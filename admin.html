📱 When a Player Requests a Slot via WhatsApp
1 Receive the booking request. The player will send a pre-filled message via the public hub. It includes their Gamer Tag, Branch, Station Type, and Duration.
2 Check availability above. Confirm the requested station type has an open slot on this admin panel before proceeding.
3 Send the UPI payment link. Use your WhatsApp /qr quick-reply shortcut to instantly share your UPI QR code or payment link. Wait for payment confirmation.
4 Confirm payment received. Once paid, reply to the player:
✅ *Slot Confirmed!* Welcome to G.O.A.T 🎮 Your [STATION] is ready. Walk in and head to [Station ID]. See you in the Lobby! GGz 🔥
5 Mark the station as BOOKED here. Click the station button above to toggle it to Booked. Enter the "Until" time (session end time) so the public hub shows it correctly.
6 When session ends, click the station again to toggle it back to Available. The public hub updates instantly.

💡 Tip: Save the message template above as a WhatsApp Quick Reply (Business App → Tools → Quick Replies → /confirmed) so you can send it in one tap.
